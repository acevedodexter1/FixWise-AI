Did the sound stop after you connected headphones?
Yes No
Disconnect the headphones, then check whether the speaker icon shows an output device.
Why: Windows can keep sending audio to a device that is no longer plugged in.

How it works

  1. Describe it. Type the problem in your own words, or upload a screenshot of the error.
  2. Answer a few questions. The next question depends on your last answer.
  3. Try one safe step at a time. Tell us what happened, or that you already tried it.
  4. Learn what happened. Get a report, plus a plain explanation of the cause.

When it tells you to stop

If a problem needs opening the device, board-level checks, or electrical testing, FixWise AI will not guide you through it. It will recommend a technician and prepare a summary you can hand over.
